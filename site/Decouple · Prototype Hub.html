The journey

One path, two people, from first search to sealed order.

Every prototype is a stage of the same story. They share one state, so what Sarah enters in her interview carries through to the order the court seals. Mark, her ex, is played by the system so each step can be demoed solo.

MVP 01DisclosureReady for testing

Shared statementFinancial disclosure, without a bank connection

The open financial statement mediators ask for, built by the couple. Each person enters exact figures and where they came from. Then both sides sit in one document: what’s missing or doesn’t match is flagged and sorted out in comments. When both agree, it produces an open financial statement, the figures for a D81, or a Form E draft.

Journey
Start→Your side (wizard)→Your statement→Safety check & invite→Shared statement→Both agree→Documents

  • The two-sided document from Protos 07 and 09, with comments
  • The full vision’s screens, type and colours

  • Settlement proposals, court filing and the dashboard
  • The mediator’s view, which comes later

MVP 01Questions & assumptions

Shared statementWhat we need to learn before building it

Assumptions
  • Couples will give exact figures, with dates and documents, without a bank connection doing it for them
  • A shared document with flags beats spreadsheets by email, enough to pay for
  • The person who starts will invite their partner, and the partner will fill in their side
  • Mediators and solicitors will accept a statement the couple built themselves
  • “Missing” flags, from “what do you think they have?”, catch the things that unravel agreements
Key questions
  • UXWhere do people stall on accuracy: finding figures, dates, or documents?
  • UXDoes “fill in yours before you see theirs” feel fair, or like being kept in the dark?
  • UXIs “what do you think they have?” helpful, or does it feel like spying?
  • BusinessIs this worth paying for on its own, and how much? Per couple, or per person?
  • BusinessDoes it lead into the full journey, or replace it for simpler cases?
  • LegalCan a couple-built, both-confirmed statement stand in for a mediator’s open financial statement? What wording should the “both satisfied” step use?
Gaps to think through
  • The partner’s own journey: arriving from an invite, on a phone
  • Sign-up and accounts: where they sit, and when we ask
  • Secure document storage, and deleting it afterwards
  • Updating figures after both have agreed
MVP bet
We believe

A shared, flagged statement gets couples to complete disclosure faster than spreadsheets, with fewer gaps

We’ll test it with

Moderated sessions as Sarah, next to Protos 06–07, plus five mediator interviews

We’re right if

≥70% finish their side with exact figures, ≥4/5 on usefulness of the flags, and mediators say they’d accept it
