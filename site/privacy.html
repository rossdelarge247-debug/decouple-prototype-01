Privacy notice

How we look after your information.

The free guide is made on your device. We only receive your answers if you say yes, and even then we never receive your name, your email address, or your answers to the safety questions.

Your guide is saved in your own browser. No account, no name needed.
If you agree, your answers come to us anonymously, to help us build a better service.
You can say no and get exactly the same guide.
No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies.
Our emails never mention separation in the subject line.

The toolkit, in testing sessions

If you test the toolkit prototypes with a tester code:

  • What you type into a tool isn’t sent to us. We receive your answers to the questions at the end of each part, and which buttons you pressed, for example “Connect your bank”, or the price you were shown for a pack. Your “Is it right for us?” answers are never sent: only which of the three results you saw.
  • Ask and the journal companion send what you type to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI model, to write a reply. We don’t store it, and Anthropic doesn’t use it to train its models. [confirm Anthropic’s API retention period]
  • Prices are a test. Different people see different prices. No payment is ever taken, and we never ask for card details.

Cookies and storage on your device

We only use storage that’s needed for the site to work, so we don’t ask for cookie consent. [confirm under PECR reg. 6]

  • Your guide and progress, in your browser’s local storage, so you can come back to it. Clear your browser data to remove it, or use “Start again” in your guide.
  • Answers waiting to be sent, only if you said yes, until our server confirms it has them.
  • A session cookie if you use a tester code, and a short-lived security cookie after the “are you a person?” check.

Who else handles it

These companies process information for us, under contracts that only let them use it to provide their service:

  • Google: stores answers and sign-ups in our spreadsheet.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Resend: sends the guide email.
  • Cloudflare: the check that you’re a person (Turnstile).
  • Anthropic: writes the replies in Ask and the journal companion.

Some of these process information in the United States. [transfer mechanism: UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, or the IDTA, per provider]

How long we keep it

  • Guide answers: 12 months after our research ends, then deleted or made fully anonymous.
  • Sign-up emails: until you unsubscribe, or 12 months after launch, whichever is sooner.
  • Guide email: we don’t keep it. [provider log retention]

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, ask us to stop using it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing doesn’t affect anything we did before.

  • In your guide: switch off “Send my answers” under Your privacy. Nothing more is sent.
  • To delete what we have: email hello@decouple.uk with your guide ID (shown under Your privacy). Your answers are anonymous, so this ID is the only way we can find them.
  • To unsubscribe: use the link in any email, or write to us.

We’ll reply within one month. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Your safety

If someone else uses your device or reads your email, use the Quick exit button, a private browsing window, and an email address they can’t see. Our emails never mention separation in the subject line. If you’re worried about your safety, call the National Domestic Abuse Helpline free on 0808 2000 247, any time. In an emergency, call 999.
