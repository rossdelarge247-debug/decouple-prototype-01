M03CollateralNeeds an accuracy check first

Help & supportA free public page at decouple.uk/help, the same as MVP 08

Free help for anyone separating in England and Wales: safety first, then fourteen topics in the order a separation runs, with financial disclosure explained and a hand-off to the toolkit. GOV.UK text is reused under its licence and fetched when the site builds; charities are linked, never copied.

What’s on it
  • Safety now, with the helplines, always first
  • Per topic: our short summary, the GOV.UK pages, more help and where to get advice
  • Financial disclosure: the three routes, the forms, and what to gather
  • Quick exit, the licence line and “not legal advice”
What it tells us
Search traffic, once indexedSign-ups from “Start your financial statement”
Before it’s public: a family solicitor or mediator checks it, and the indexing switch goes on. Reading it sends nothing; only the sign-up does.Open the page
