About Decouple

Made with people who’ve been through it.

Separating is hard enough. Working out what to do next, and in what order, shouldn’t make it harder.

Most people find out the hard way. Forms nobody explains. Advice that pulls in different directions. Weeks spent working out what comes first, while the rest of life carries on around you.

Decouple is a small team, working with a group of people who’ve been through separation and divorce themselves. They tell us what was hardest, what they wish they’d known, and where they got stuck. Everything we make is shaped by them, and tested with them first.

We’re building the help they wish they’d had: clear steps, in the right order, for England and Wales.

What we’re not

Not solicitors

We don’t give legal advice. We’ll tell you plainly when it’s worth talking to someone who does.

Not taking sides

Decouple is for either of you, or both of you together.

Not charging for the guide

The guide is free, and you don’t need an account to use it.
